## TL;DR — 3-Tier Launch Ranking (from-MY indie perspective)

Optimised for: high waste (demand), high smartphone penetration (TAM), English-friendly UI, weak/no inventory-tracking incumbent. Surplus-rescue apps (TGTG-style) are a different category — they don't compete with a pantry tracker.

| Tier | Countries | Rationale (inferred from data below) |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — strong candidates** | **Australia, UK, US, Singapore, Malaysia (home)** | High waste (73-98 kg/yr), 80-97% smartphone, English-native, no dominant inventory-tracker incumbent. AU + UK + US are top-5 app-spender markets. SG/MY = home-base testbed with highest APAC penetration and the founder's local knowledge. |
| **Tier 2 — secondary candidates** | UAE, Saudi Arabia, Netherlands, Sweden, Denmark, Germany | High waste (59-105 kg/yr), 80-95% smartphone. Strong English fluency. EU markets are saturated with surplus-rescue apps (TGTG, Karma, Phenix, Olio) but inventory-tracking niche is open. UAE/KSA = high spend, but Arabic localisation needed and Barakah dominates the surplus-rescue lane. |
| **Tier 3 — skip for now** | Japan, South Korea, France, Thailand, Indonesia, Philippines | Skip reasons differ: **JP/KR** = waste already structurally low (60/95 kg) due to RFID pay-as-you-throw and Mottainai culture, dominant local-language incumbents (Tabete with 1.2M users in JP), language barrier. **FR** = Phenix dominates surplus-rescue, French-only UI required. **TH/ID/PH** = lower app-store spend (volume-only markets), 26-86 kg waste but lower willingness-to-pay for productivity apps; ID/PH smartphone penetration weaker (43-58%). |

## Key cross-cutting observations

1. **Surplus-rescue ≠ inventory-tracking.** Almost every "incumbent" listed is a TGTG-style B2C surplus marketplace. GroceryApp is a pantry tracker — different category, no direct competition in 14 of 17 markets. Closest direct rivals: **NoWaste (DK)** + long-tail US apps (Pantry Check, Fridgely) — none dominant.
2. **UNEP confidence.** Only AU, JP, UK, US, EU, KSA + Canada have "high confidence" household estimates [^uw-conf]. SG/MY/TH/ID/PH/UAE figures are modeled — directionally useful, not precise.
3. **Hot-climate signal.** UNEP 2024: hotter countries skew higher per-capita waste (fresh-food consumption + cold-chain gaps) [^uw-conf]. Reinforces SG/MY/TH/UAE/KSA figures despite lower income.
4. **Spend vs volume.** US/JP/UK/AU are spender markets (paid-tier viable). ID/PH/TH/MY lean volume (ad-supported freemium). KSA/UAE = high per-user spend, smaller TAM.
5. **Language gating.** English-comfortable: SG, MY, AU, UK, US, NL, SE, DK, UAE (bilingual). Localisation needed: JP, KR, FR, DE, TH, ID, PH, KSA.
