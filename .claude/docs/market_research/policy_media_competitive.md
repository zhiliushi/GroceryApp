# Food Waste — Policy, Media & Competitive Landscape

Scope: 17 countries. Loudness scored 0-3 (0 = silent, 3 = load-bearing legislation/enforcement). Sources cited inline.

## 1. Government / regulatory attention by country

| Country | Score | Key instruments / status |
|---|---|---|
| **France** | 3 | 2016 Garot Law: supermarkets ≥400 m² banned from destroying unsold food; must donate. Donations +22% Y1, ~30% by 2017; >90% of supermarkets donating by 2018 (vs 66% pre-law). Extended to catering/processing (2018 EGalim) and wholesale (2020 AGEC). [ZWE](https://zerowasteeurope.eu/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/zwe_11_2020_factsheet_france_en.pdf) · [OECD 2025](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-issue-focus/food-loss-and-waste/A%20Stocktaking%20of%20FLW-France.pdf) |
| **South Korea** | 3 | Volume-based food-waste fee since 1995, RFID weighing from 2013; 14% household reduction; processing cost ₩823.5B in 2024. Seoul "reduction points" cash-reward 2024. [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10638389) · [Dev Asia](https://development.asia/explainer/using-fee-based-system-reduce-waste) |
| **UK** | 3 | Courtauld Commitment 2030 (WRAP) — 50% vs 2007; 18% below baseline but per-capita +5.6% recent; 25% of large F&D firms have targets (2024). [WRAP Progress 23-24](https://www.wrap.ngo/resources/report/courtauld-commitment-2030-annual-progress-report-2023-24) |

## 5. Whitespace assessment by tier

**Loud tier (policy 3 + media 3) — UK, France, Australia, Denmark, US**
- **UK:** NoWaste/Kitche present; CozZo sunsetting Dec 2025 leaves a vacancy. Whitespace for barcode+manual simple-tier inventory under NoWaste's annual price.
- **France:** FrigoMagic + Phenix dominant; both adjacent (recipe / surplus). Inventory gap exists; French localization cost.
- **Australia:** TGTG present; no native inventory app surfaced. Whitespace likely; small market (<30M).
- **Denmark/Sweden:** NoWaste home market; saturated.
- **US:** Largest by revenue. AnyList/Out of Milk own list space; Pantry Check/Your Food fragment inventory. Crowded but no dominant inventory player.

**Medium tier — Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Germany, Netherlands, UAE**
- **JP/KR:** Surplus-rescue saturated; inventory-tracking is whitespace. High localization cost (script + local barcode DBs).
- **Singapore:** 2024 RSA is B2B; consumer apps thin; English-friendly; ~6M market.
- **UAE:** Ne'ma fresh + state-funded; English+Arabic; no local inventory app; premium ARPU.
- **Germany/Netherlands:** Bring! owns list space; inventory gap.

**Quiet tier — Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Philippines, Saudi Arabia**
- **Malaysia:** Home market. Zero direct competitor surfaced. Earned-media hook via Ramadhan rescue stories. Low CAC but low willingness-to-pay for behavioural SaaS.
- **Indonesia:** Big population; weak consumer-app monetization here; Bappenas roadmap may build awareness 2025+.
- **Thailand/Philippines:** No legislation; small paid-app TAM until policy lands.
- **Saudi Arabia:** State-driven narrative; Arabic-localized inventory whitespace; premium but payment/cultural barriers.

**Net:** UK (post-CozZo), Australia, Singapore, UAE all present whitespace for an inventory-tracking app at the RM 5.99 / RM 12.99 price point. NoWaste is the price-anchor — match or undercut. Differentiation lever: the "what I used vs threw" learning loop, which no listed competitor emphasizes.
